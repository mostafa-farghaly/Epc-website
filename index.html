Services

Premium services powered by decades of expertise.

1

Outdoor MV/LV Substations

The prefabricated substations provide a particularly interesting and optimized solution regarding delivery time, construction works, erection works, and commissioning.

2

Underground P.Cables

Undergrounding is the replacement of overhead cables providing electrical power or telecommunications with underground cables.

3

Transmission Lines

These lines have an overhead cable length greater than 50 km and less than 150 km, with allowable voltages ranging from 20 to 100 kV.

4

Indoor P. Stations

Reduce the noise from transformers, improve appearance, or protect switchgear from extreme climate or pollution.

5

Distribution P.Panels

Distribution panels can either be large or small. The amount of circuits depends on the needs of the location that is being powered.

6

Lighting Devices & Accessories

While the bulbs and the light fixture are one of the main concerns when it comes to lighting, you should still not disregard the other accessories. These devices improve efficiency and the overall experience.

7

Light Current Systems

Fire Alarm Systems, Burglar Alarm System, Access Control System, CCTV, Parking & Gate Systems, Time Attendance, Data Networks, Intercoms, SMATV, BMS, and more.

8

Sub-Main P.Panels

The main panel is the breaker box that takes the initial connection of electricity. It’s the first line of defense against surges and protects both people and equipment.

9

Landscape & Street Lighting

Landscape lighting enhances outdoor spaces, improving safety, aesthetics, and accessibility for gardens, streets, and public areas.

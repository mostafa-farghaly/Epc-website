About Us

Electrical Projects Contracting (EPC)

EPC – Electrical Projects Contracting is a distinguished member of the Pioneers Paths Company (PPC) Alliance in Saudi Arabia, delivering excellence in electrical contracting solutions. Since its establishment in 2004, EPC has been dedicated to providing reliable, high-quality electrical infrastructure services that comply with the highest local and international standards.

EPC began its journey by executing electrical networks, power systems, and substation projects for private sector clients. Over the years, the company expanded its capabilities through strategic collaborations with public entities and governmental authorities, following its registration with the General Authority for Investment (GAFI) and the Egyptian Federation for Construction and Building Contractors in 2014.

Today, as part of the Pioneers Paths Company (PPC) Alliance, EPC is recognized as a leading provider of medium and low voltage networks, main power supply systems, and comprehensive electrical solutions. Our services are delivered with precision, professionalism, and strict adherence to safety, quality, and sustainability standards, reinforcing our commitment to shaping future-ready infrastructure across the region.

Our Mission

To deliver safe, reliable, and innovative electrical contracting solutions that meet the highest standards of quality and performance—ensuring customer satisfaction and contributing to sustainable infrastructure development in Egypt and beyond.

Our Vision

To be recognized as one of the region’s leading electrical contracting companies, trusted for our technical excellence, on-time delivery, and unwavering commitment to safety, quality, and professionalism.

Teamwork & Excellence

Our strength lies in our people. EPC’s team of dedicated engineers, supervisors, and skilled technicians collaborates seamlessly to ensure every project is executed safely, efficiently, and to the highest standards. Together, we transform challenges into successful, sustainable results.
